A1

Extreme Catch

↑

Long-toss accuracy with a built-in distance progression.

▶ Watch · 0:09

Setup

  • Pair players up
  • 4 cones in a line: 3 close cones spaced 5 ft apart · 4th cone 20 ft beyond
  • One partner stands holding a ball at the far cone, the other at the first close cone

How to Play

  • On go, throw to your partner
  • Each clean catch = step back one cone
  • After throwing from the last (farthest) cone, start moving forward again
  • Each pair gets a turn, then switch roles
Coaching tip Beginners may not reach their partner on the fly — that's OK. Cue them to use their whole body when throwing, not just their arm.
A2

Lazy Catch

↑

Read grounders from a low knees-down stance.

▶ Watch · 1:23

Setup

  • Groups of 5 · coach 15 ft from the front of the line
  • All 5 players on both knees (don't sit on heels)
  • Glove fingertips on the ground, ready

How to Play

  • Roll a grounder to each player in turn
  • They field & throw back without standing or sitting
  • Work down the line, then back with backhanded grounders
  • +1 per clean field — beat last round's score
Coaching tip Two hands every time. Snap the top hand down on the ball like an alligator. Backhand plays = scoop & lift. Both hands is the only way to make it to the show.
A3

Alligator Traps

↑

Gobble up grounders with proper two-hand technique.

▶ Watch · 2:36

Setup

  • Team in a line · coach 10 ft away
  • First player in set stance: knees bent, hips low

How to Play

  • Roll a grounder; fielder receives with both hands — top hand chomps like an alligator
  • Throw back, 3 grounders per player, then go to the back of the line
  • Track clean fields — beat the score next round
Coaching tip Eyes on the ball all the way into the glove. The better they see it, the less likely it is to scoot past them.
A4

Base Path Relay

↑

Conditioning + rounding-the-bag form, race style.

▶ Watch · 3:41

Setup

  • Two equal teams, each team split in half
  • One half of each team at home plate · the other half at 2nd base

How to Play

  • On go, the first two runners take off from home — one via 1st, the other via 3rd
  • High-five the next teammate waiting at 2nd
  • That teammate takes off the opposite way back to home
  • First team to complete all legs wins
Coaching tip There's a difference between fast and quick. Anyone can learn to be quick — round the bag with head down, arms pumping, foot on the inside corner.
A5

Blast Ball

↑

Low-stakes, high-fun scrimmage — first taste of the game.

▶ Watch · 4:57

Setup

  • Split team: one group at bat, one in the field (no gloves needed)
  • Coach pitches for both teams
  • Use a plastic bat & practice ball

How to Play (modified rules)

  • No strikeouts — hitter keeps swinging until they make contact
  • Innings end only when every hitter has batted
  • Any ball that reaches outfield grass = home run
  • Most runs after 3 innings = Blast Ball World Champions
Coaching tip Bigger ball + no strikeouts = newbies feel safe and successful. Crossing home plate is just as sweet — celebrate every run.
A6

Freeze or Tag-Up

↑

Read the ball off the bat — go on a grounder, freeze & tag up on a fly.

▶ Watch · 7:22

Setup

  • One volunteer plays left field
  • Rest of team lines up by 3rd base
  • Coach stands between 3rd and the mound with a bucket of balls

How to Play

  • First player steps onto 3rd in set position
  • Coach simulates a hit — either a grounder OR a fly ball to left field
  • Runner decides: take off for home on a grounder · freeze + tag up on a fly (wait for the catch before advancing)
  • +1 per correct decision
Coaching tip Knowing when & how to tag up is the difference between getting cut down on the bases and scoring a run. Get the line involved — ask if their teammate tagged up properly, and why.
A7

Gold Glove

↑

Full-team fielding workout — every position, in-game scenarios.

▶ Watch · 8:36

Setup

  • Whole team takes the field, one player per position
  • Coach with a bat & bucket near the mound

How to Play (run through the sequence)

  • Outfield, 2 balls each → throw to cutoff on the way to 2nd (SS for L/CF, 2B for RF)
  • Outfield, 2 more → throw home, one to cutoff, one on the fly
  • Infield grounders → record the out at 1st (1B throws to 3rd)
  • Double plays → 4-6-3 from 2nd, then 5-2-3 from home
  • Finish with pop-ups to the catcher
  • Call out scenarios (runners on, outs, score) before each play
Coaching tip Defense is mind as much as body. Where to throw depends on the runners, the outs, and the score. Keep them dialed in by changing scenarios every rep.
A8

Base Path Blitz

↑

Every base-running scenario, on your call.

▶ Watch · 9:57

Setup

  • Line team up behind home plate
  • Coach in foul territory near a base path (2nd coach can cover the other side)

How to Play

  • Player steps into the batter's box — no bat
  • On your call, they fake-swing & take off
  • Approaching 1st: you tell them run through · turn & look · round to 2nd
  • Approaching 2nd: you tell them stay · go to 3rd · round home
  • +1 every time they touch the plate
Coaching tip Runners don't watch the ball — they listen to the coach. Their job is run hard and react.
A9

Pitcher's Poison

↑

Beginner-friendly scrimmage — get the out by throwing to the pitcher.

▶ Watch · 11:27

Setup

  • Split team in half — one at bat, one in the field
  • Coach pitches for both teams from the mound

How to Play (one rule twist)

  • Play a normal scrimmage with one change:
  • Defense gets the out by throwing the ball back to the pitcher before the runner reaches the base
  • Set number of innings, then swap teams
Coaching tip Beginners don't have to think about complex defensive scenarios — just field cleanly and get the ball back to you. For more advanced players, it's a "back to basics" reset that puts all the focus on hitting.
