01

Flipper

↑

Refines glove work with a flick of the wrist.

Setup

  • Circle up team, players ~3 ft apart
  • Give one player a ball
  • Everyone in ready position, throwing hand behind back

How to Play

  • On your call, player flips the ball to anyone in the circle
  • Receive & flip again as quickly as possible — no drops
  • Drop the ball = step out
  • Last fielder standing wins the round
Coaching cue Flip at knee height — keeps the ball low & close to the base for quick, easy tags. Stay athletic, move your feet.
02

Rundown (Pickle)

↑

Trap the runner between the bags.

Setup

  • Groups of 3: two fielders, one runner
  • Fielders on 2nd & 3rd, ball starts at 2nd
  • Runner stands 15 ft off the bag

How to Play

  • Fielders throw back and forth, trying to tag the runner
  • Runner escapes by reaching a base safely OR forcing more than 3 throws
  • Scoring: +1 if runner is tagged back at 2nd · 0 if runner reaches 3rd
  • Play to 5 points, then rotate
Real-game tip Runners should avoid getting caught — but if trapped, stay alive as long as possible so other runners can advance.
03

Ready Aim Bunt

↑

Small-ball accuracy — bunt into target zones.

Setup

  • Two 5×5 ft cone squares down the 1st & 3rd base lines
  • Squares start ~5 ft from home, extend 5 ft into fair territory
  • Coach 15 ft from home with bucket of balls
  • Hitter at plate with bat & helmet

How to Play

  • Coach tosses a pitch
  • Hitter bunts, aiming for one of the squares
  • Each hitter gets several reps — track on-target bunts
  • Challenge them to beat their score next time
Form check Feet square to pitcher · bat head above the handle · stay back, don't drift forward.
04

Knock It Off

↑

Throwing accuracy — knock the ball off the tee.

Setup

  • Line team up behind a bucket of balls
  • Tee 20 ft away with a soccer or volleyball balanced on top

How to Play

  • First in line throws, trying to knock the ball off
  • Scoring: 2 pts clean knock-off · 1 pt hitting the tee
  • Most points after 5 rounds wins
Coaching cue Don't let competitiveness break form — step and throw. Striding toward your target = accurate throws in a real game.
05

Grab Bag

↑

Alternate bare-handed grounders and pop-ups.

Setup

  • Two cones 10 ft apart in the outfield
  • Split team in half, line up behind each cone
  • Coach stands ~15 ft in front, between the lines, with a bucket

How to Play

  • Round 1: grounders to one line, pop-ups to the other
  • Field the ball, throw back, move to end of the other line
  • Round 2: mix grounders & pop-ups between both lines
  • +1 per clean field — challenge them to beat their round-1 score
Reinforce Always use two hands — even on foam balls. That's the habit that keeps them off the blooper reel.
06

Line Drive Derby

↑

Pairs work together to rope line drives off the tee.

Setup

  • Two tees 10 ft apart inside the batting cage
  • Pair up: one hitter, one feeder (both wear helmets)
  • Hitters in stance facing away from each other; feeders kneel beside a bucket

How to Play

  • Feeders place ball on tee; hitters swing for the back of the cage
  • Feeder reloads only when safe
  • Feeder counts line drives
  • After 1 min, switch roles. After 2 min, most line drives wins
  • Rotate so every pair competes
Don't rush Faster swing ≠ better contact. Cue: level, compact, controlled.
07

Mind the Gap

↑

Outfielders call for fly balls — loudly.

Setup

  • Two cones in the outfield, ~30 ft apart
  • Split team into two lines, one behind each cone
  • Coach stands between the lines, ~20 ft away, with a ball

How to Play

  • One line = primary fielders · other line = backups
  • Lob a high ball directly between the two cones
  • Both run to center · primary calls for it · backup peels off but stays ready
  • Whoever catches it throws back. Next pair up
  • Swap primary/backup roles and play again
Coaching cue There's no such thing as calling too loud. Silent fielders = collisions and dropped balls.
08

Throwing Seeds

↑

Outfielders cut runners down at the plate.

Setup

  • Trash can on its side at home plate, opening facing the field
  • Team lined up in the outfield
  • Coach 50 ft away with bat & bucket of balls

How to Play

  • First in line gets in ready position, knees bent, feet alive
  • Coach hits a ball — fielder fields it, throws into the can
  • Mix grounders & fly balls across rounds
  • Most throws in the trash wins
Form check Elbow up · glove pointed at target · let it fly.
09

The Box

↑

Fly-ball tracking from the middle of the box.

Setup

  • 4 cones form a large rectangle · 5th cone in the middle
  • Coach stands at the edge of the space with a bucket

How to Play

  • First player starts at the center cone facing coach
  • Coach throws a shallow pop-up to one of the four corners
  • Fielder runs, tracks, and catches using proper footwork
  • Throw back, get 3 attempts before next player is up
  • +1 per catch — beat your score next round
Coaching cue Turn your belt buckle toward the ball and sprint to where you expect it to land. Explode in any direction as soon as it leaves the bat.
10

Tunnel Ball

↑

Hit frozen ropes straight up the middle.

Setup

  • Two lines of 5 cones each — form a 5 ft tunnel, wider on one end
  • Split team into hitters & fielders
  • Hitters line up at the narrow end; fielders spread inside the tunnel
  • Coach beside hitters with a bucket

How to Play

  • Soft-toss to first hitter
  • Run: ball lands inside tunnel, not cleanly fielded
  • Out: fielders catch a fly · cleanly field a grounder · ball lands outside cones
  • 3 outs = switch sides
  • Most runs after 3 innings wins
The fundamental Drive the ball up the middle. It's the highest-percentage contact zone against any pitch.
