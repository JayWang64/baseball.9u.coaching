01

Mind the Gap

↑

Tracking fly balls + loud, clear calls between two fielders.

▶ Watch · 0:13

Setup

  • 2 cones in the outfield, ~30 ft apart
  • Team splits into two equal lines, one at each cone
  • Coach stands 20 ft in front, between the cones, with a ball

How to Play

  • One line = primary fielders · the other = backups
  • Lob a ball high & right down the middle of the gap
  • Both players sprint to center — primary calls it loud, backup peels off but stays ready
  • Fielder throws ball back; next pair goes
  • After everyone's had a turn, swap roles
Coaching tip There's no such thing as calling too loudly. Stay silent and they'll collide. Game day is noisy — train them to own the call over the parents and the PA.
02

Right On Target

↑

Pitching to spots — corners, down Broadway, low.

▶ Watch · 4:10

Setup

  • Pitchers line up behind the mound
  • Coach catches behind the plate
  • Each pitcher gets 12 pitches per round

How to Play

  • Throw 2 pitches each to imaginary targets, in order:
  • Up & away · down & away · up & in · down & in
  • Then one right down Broadway, finish middle-low
  • +1 point per strike on target — beat your high score next round
Coaching tip If they're missing spots, have them point the glove arm at the target. That steadies the line of sight and keeps their head from flying open.
03

Four Corners

↑

Accurate throws around the diamond, in rhythm.

▶ Watch · 5:13

Setup

  • 4 groups, one at each base · everyone has a glove
  • First player in each group stands on the bag
  • Catcher starts with the ball

How to Play

  • On go, ball goes: catcher → 1st → 2nd → 3rd → home
  • Catch · turn · step · throw — all the way around
  • After 2 rounds, reverse: catcher → 2nd → 1st → 3rd → home
  • Count consecutive throws without a drop — beat last group's streak
Coaching tip The whip around the horn is all about the transfer — glove to hand in one fluid motion. In a real game that's the difference between the out and a run scoring.
04

Two Ball

↑

Barehand catching — confidence when the glove isn't an option.

▶ Watch · 6:32

Setup

  • Players form a circle, equidistant, no gloves
  • Coach in the middle with two balls, one in each hand

How to Play

  • Make eye contact, underhand both balls simultaneously
  • Player catches one in each hand, underhands them both back
  • Track the streak — beat it next time
  • Once they've got it, volunteers rotate into the middle
Coaching tip Kids would rather catch with a glove — but they won't always have the option. Think awkward feed turning two, or a hot grounder off the chest. Bare-handing the ball is a real skill.
05

Knockout

↑

Pitching under pressure — paint the corner or get knocked out.

▶ Watch · 7:42

Setup

  • Pitchers line up behind the mound · coach catches behind plate
  • First player gets a ball, climbs the mound

How to Play

  • Throw a strike → safe, go to the back of the line
  • Throw a ball → you're on the hot seat, next pitcher is up
  • Next pitcher throws a strike → previous pitcher is OUT
  • Next pitcher throws a ball → hot seat flips back
  • Last ace standing wins
Coaching tip Good pitching isn't about throwing hard. Painting the corner beats a blazing fastball — even in the majors. Reward the kid who lives on the edges.
06

Soft Toss Slugging

↑

Bridge from tee work to hitting a moving ball.

▶ Watch · 8:50

Setup

  • Coach kneels ~4 ft to the side of the plate with a bucket
  • Hitter steps in with a balanced batting stance

How to Play

  • Soft-toss into the hitting zone — 10 swings per hitter
  • Smooth, quality swings — not max effort
  • Hitter counts solid hits in a row, then beats it next round
Coaching tip The #1 fix at this age: don't pull the head out. Tell them to keep their chin tucked to the front shoulder and watch the ball hit the bat. Head still = more contact, every time.
07

Throwing Seeds

↑

Outfield throws to the plate — cut runners down.

▶ Watch · 10:02

Setup

  • Trash can on its side at home plate, opening facing the field
  • Team lines up in the outfield
  • Coach 50 ft away with a bat & bucket

How to Play

  • First fielder in ready position — legs moving, knees bent
  • Hit them a ball (mix grounders and fly balls)
  • They field it & throw toward home, trying to land it in the can
  • Next fielder up · most balls in the can wins
Coaching tip Form first, heat second. Cue them every rep: elbow up, glove pointed at the target, let it fly. Sloppy mechanics = sailed throw and a run scores.
08

Pop Quiz

↑

Whole-team pop-ups — catch a target number in a row, or start over.

▶ Watch · 11:13

Setup

  • Team takes the field — one player per position
  • Coach stands in front of home with a bucket
  • Pick a target number of consecutive catches (e.g., 5)

How to Play

  • Throw fly balls around the field at random
  • Every catch counts toward the target
  • Anyone drops it — back to zero. Whole team starts over
  • Hit the target = team wins the round
Coaching tip Two hands, every catch. Kids will try to one-hand it like the pros — remind them the only way to make it to the show is by securing the ball. And call it loud: "ball, ball, ball" — even when no one else is close.
